# Separate ideas and project incubation space

On 2026-09-07, the maintainer closed the proposal to create a separate ideas and project-incubation space from urgent Battle Plan work. The initiative had not reached a concrete product direction or an actionable scope, so it is not being pursued in its current form.

This does not prevent a future, more specific request for capturing or reviewing ideas. A future proposal should define the intended surface, the distinction from Battle Plan Tasks and Projects, and the promotion workflow before implementation is considered.

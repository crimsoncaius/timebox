# Retain Quick Tracking failures separately from diagnostic details

Accepted 2026-10-29. Android Quick Tracking retains failed attempts, original words when captured, and readable failure reasons in Assistant Review on Android and web. Capture failures say “No transcript captured.” Unknown recording outcomes remain uncertain until reconciled; diagnostics and reconnection never authorize execution. This extends ADR 0014's explicit-retry rule and ADR 0012's separation of product history from tracing.

Retry is available only on the originating installation and original server, because that installation may hold an unsynced operation. Before retrying, reconcile its durable activity journal and server receipt. Preserve the original time reference, show the interpreted activity and time for confirmation, and revalidate activity state at confirmation. An expired command or day boundary offers Day correction rather than shifting its meaning. Duplicate taps cannot produce duplicate recordings. Speech replacement and retry attempts remain grouped with the original command; successful recovery links to the recorded result. Dismissal clears attention without deleting history. Informational no-change results are distinct from failures.

Review on another device explains that recovery requires the originating phone, with Day correction and dismissal available. A changed server never receives the old command or diagnostics. Reinstallation does not grant ownership of earlier attempts. Speech failures offer Speak again on the originating phone; web explains where to do this.

Upload diagnostic events automatically, queued offline and independent of recording: failure stage, exception type and available stack frames, request ID, app version and Android version. Do not collect audio, credentials, unrelated activity, or raw HTTP bodies. Keep original commands and readable outcomes without automatic expiry; remove technical diagnostics after 30 days. Backend interpretation failures must be captured as well as client failures. Retry history remains available after resolution or dismissal.

Scope is Android Quick Tracking recovery with shared Review visibility, not general application crash reporting. Unavailable origin devices require manual correction; cross-device command execution is deliberately excluded to avoid duplicating phone-local activity.

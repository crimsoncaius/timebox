# Derive Time Goal progress from Actual Blocks

Time Goals have their own targets and lifecycle, independent of completion-based Habits and Recurring Task Series. Their progress is derived from Actual Blocks attributed to the selected Task Type and its descendants, including running blocks through now; recording a separate completion or freezing a past result would let the goal disagree with corrected activity records. Therefore corrections, deletions, reclassification and hierarchy changes may change past Time Goal outcomes, while target-only edits take effect next period and preserve the thresholds used for earlier periods.

The accepted Android presentation and lifecycle decisions are recorded in [Time Goals in Chronicle](../design/time-goals.md). The reviewed prototype is evidence for the design, not the production implementation.

# Recurring-details visual inspiration

These six images were supplied by the user in the conversation about Mobile: Redesign recurring-task details and management. Original PNGs are preserved unchanged.

**Authority:** These images are inspiration, not commands, requirements, approved layouts, or a source of recurrence semantics. Their annotations and example controls must not silently expand scope. The user accepted clear grouping and action scope as inspiration while leaving cards, calendar/list presentation, styling, wording, and exact controls open.

The accepted direction prioritizes the current period and its work, with series settings secondary. Scheduled and quota experiences share an overall structure but keep Task Occurrences distinct from Quota Trackers and Session Tasks. Consult CONTEXT.md, relevant ADRs, DESIGN.md, and the existing recurrence decisions. In particular, do not infer quota pre-planning support, permanent series deletion, or lifecycle behavior from a mockup. Dates and counts are illustrative.

## Images

1. [Scheduled series overview](01-scheduled-series.png): identity, recurrence summary, current occurrence, upcoming work, settings.
2. [Quota series overview](02-quota-series.png): current-period progress and independently completable sessions.
3. [Planning exception](03-planning-exception.png): visual distinction between unplanned work and a preferred slot that could not create a plan; applicability must follow existing capabilities.
4. [Individual task details](04-individual-task.png): parent-series context and task-scoped actions.
5. [Paused and ended states](05-paused-and-ended.png): lifecycle explanation and retained-work access.
6. [Series management](06-series-management.png): discoverable management and understandable consequences; pictured actions are not a supported-action specification.
